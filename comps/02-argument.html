AI-built marketing sites fail in predictable ways.

  1. The model designs blind — it never looks at what it rendered. fixed: a screenshot loop; nothing is presented unseen
  2. Taste evaporates between sessions. fixed: a written brief — PRODUCT.md and DESIGN.md, decided by you
  3. One-shot generation converges to the category’s median. fixed: divergent comps and a type audition before commitment
  4. “Done” gets declared without anything being verified. fixed: four quality gates, measured from the rendered DOM

site-craft is a skill that runs your coding agent the way a small studio runs a job — phased, user-gated, screenshot-verified. You are the art director; the agent brings options and screenshots, you bring verdicts.
